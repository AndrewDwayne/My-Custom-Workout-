# 10-Week Muscle-Building Program
**Goal:** Build muscle | **Location:** Home | **Style:** Circuit | **Frequency:** 3x/week, 45 min | **Equipment:** Dumbbells (up to 25 lb) + resistance bands | **Cardio:** Treadmill

---

## Training Approach

Circuit-style training and muscle-building can work well together, but there's a real constraint here worth naming: at only 45 minutes and a 25 lb dumbbell cap, straight low-rep/heavy-rest strength work won't be the most efficient path to growth. Instead, this program uses **density-based hypertrophy circuits** — moderate rep ranges (10-15), short rest (30-45s) between exercises, and antagonist pairings (e.g., push paired with pull) so muscles recover just enough to keep working while overall training density stays high. Since the dumbbells cap at 25 lb, progressive overload past that point comes from three levers instead of just adding weight: slower tempo, added resistance bands layered onto dumbbell movements, and reduced rest — all explained in the progression system below.

Across the week: squat, hinge, horizontal push, vertical push, horizontal pull, vertical pull, single-leg, carry, core stabilization, and rotation/anti-rotation are all covered.

## Progression System

**Rule:** If you complete all prescribed reps at the top of the range with 2+ reps in reserve for two consecutive sessions on an exercise, progress it using **one** of these (in this order of preference until dumbbells stop being the limiter):
1. **Add a resistance band** looped under the feet/around the back for dumbbell presses, rows, or squats.
2. **Slow the tempo** — 3 seconds down, 1-second pause, 1 second up.
3. **Trim rest** by 5-10 seconds between exercises (density increase).

Early weeks, if 25 lb still feels light for an exercise, just start using it — the band/tempo levers are for once dumbbell weight stops being the challenge.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | What Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1-3 | Technique, base volume | RPE 6-7, reps 12-15, learning the circuit flow |
| Progressive Overload | 4-6 | Growth stimulus | RPE 7-8, reps trend to 10-12, add bands/tempo as dumbbells cap out |
| Performance | 7-9 | Higher density | RPE 8, rest trimmed further, reps 8-12 with heavier tempo/band load |
| Deload/Test | 10 | Consolidation | Volume cut ~40%, light loads, retest rep maxes |

## Week 1 — Full Template

### Day 1: Lower Body + Horizontal Push-Pull

**Warm-Up (5 min):** 2 min brisk treadmill walk → bodyweight squats x10, glute bridges x10, arm circles x10, band pull-aparts x15.

**Circuit (3 rounds, 30-45s rest between exercises, 90s rest between rounds):**
| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat (DB) | 12-15 | Chest up, sit back |
| DB Romanian Deadlift | 12-15 | Soft knees, hinge at hips |
| DB Floor or Bench Press | 10-12 | Neutral or standard grip |
| Band or DB Row (bent-over) | 12-15 | Squeeze shoulder blades |

**Cardio finisher:** 10 min treadmill, moderate incline walk.

**Cool-Down (3 min):** Quad stretch, hamstring stretch, doorway chest stretch.

---

### Day 2: Upper Body + Core

**Warm-Up (5 min):** 2 min jumping jacks or march in place → band external rotations x15, arm swings, cat-cow x10.

**Circuit (3 rounds, 30-45s rest, 90s between rounds):**
| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DB Shoulder Press (seated or standing) | 10-12 | Full but pain-free range |
| Band Lat Pulldown or DB Row | 12-15 | Control the eccentric |
| DB Lateral Raise | 12-15 | Light, controlled — this is an isolation move |
| Plank | 30-45s | Brace, don't let hips sag |

**Cardio finisher:** 10 min treadmill intervals — 1 min brisk / 1 min easy x5.

**Cool-Down (3 min):** Shoulder cross-body stretch, child's pose, seated spinal twist.

---

### Day 3: Full Body — Single-Leg, Carry, Rotation

**Warm-Up (5 min):** 2 min brisk walk → walking lunges x10, torso twists x10/side, band pull-aparts x15.

**Circuit (3 rounds, 30-45s rest, 90s between rounds):**
| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DB Reverse Lunge | 10-12/side | Upright torso, control the descent |
| DB Farmer's Carry | 30-40m | Grip tight, walk tall |
| Band Woodchop | 10-12/side | Rotate through the torso, controlled |
| DB Hip Thrust (or glute bridge) | 12-15 | Squeeze glutes at top |

**Cardio finisher:** 12 min treadmill, steady moderate pace.

**Cool-Down (3 min):** Hip flexor stretch, glute stretch, deep breathing.

## Weeks 2-3: Foundation Continuation

Same circuits. Apply the progression rule after each session. By Week 3, most exercises should be getting a band, tempo, or rest adjustment as dumbbells stop being the limiting factor.

## Weeks 4-6: Progressive Overload Phase

- Rep target shifts to 10-12 (from 12-15), RPE 7-8.
- Rest between exercises trims to 30s flat.
- At least 2 of the 4 exercises per day should have a band or tempo modifier by Week 5.
- Add a 4th round to one circuit per week (rotate which day) to build work capacity.

## Weeks 7-9: Performance Phase

- Rep target 8-12, RPE 8.
- Rest trims to 20-30s where form allows.
- Every session should have at least one exercise using combined band + tempo for real overload since the dumbbells alone have plateaued.
- Cardio finishers get one interval day per week in addition to Day 2's intervals.

## Week 10: Deload & Testing

- Cut rounds from 3-4 down to 2, drop bands/tempo modifiers, keep reps moderate (10-12) at an easy RPE 6.
- Mid-week: retest one circuit exercise per day for max clean reps at a fixed weight (e.g., 25 lb goblet squat, 20 lb DB press) to gauge progress vs. Week 1.
- Cardio stays light, steady-state only.

## Muscle-Building Specifics

- **Weekly volume per muscle group:** aim for roughly 10-15 working sets/week per major muscle group (legs, back, chest, shoulders) — this program lands in that range across the 3 days once rounds build to 3-4.
- **Hypertrophy rep range:** 8-15, matching what's prescribed above.
- **Progression strategy:** band/tempo/rest-based overload (see Progression System) since load is capped by equipment.
- **Recovery:** each muscle group gets ~48-72 hours between direct sessions with this full-body 3x/week layout — don't add a 4th day without adjusting volume down elsewhere.

---

## Adjusting for Time

**If you only have 30 minutes:** cut to 2 rounds instead of 3, keep the cardio finisher to 5 minutes, don't skip warm-up/cool-down.

## Missed Workouts & Low-Energy Days

- **Missed a day:** skip to the next scheduled session, don't combine two days into one.
- **Missed 2+ weeks:** restart at Week 1 loads/reps for 1-2 weeks before resuming normal progression.
- **Low energy/poor sleep:** keep the session but drop to 2 rounds at RPE 6, skip the progression attempt, treat cardio as an easy walk only.

## Deload & Warning Signs

Take an extra deload or back off if you notice: joint pain (versus normal muscle fatigue), soreness lasting beyond 72 hours, declining performance across 2+ sessions despite consistent effort, or resting heart rate elevated for several days. Any of these means back off, not push through.

---

## Recovery Guidelines

- **Sleep target:** 7-9 hours.
- **Rest days:** at least one full day between sessions.
- **Mobility:** 5 min daily, especially hips and shoulders.
- **Under-recovery signs:** persistent soreness, low motivation, stalled or dropping performance.

## Nutrition Guidance (General Principles Only)

- **Protein:** ~0.7-1.0g per lb bodyweight/day — at 130-160 lb that's roughly **90-160g/day**; adjust toward the higher end for muscle-building goals.
- **Hydration:** ~0.5-1 oz per lb bodyweight daily.
- **Pre-workout:** light carb + protein 1-2 hours prior (e.g., yogurt + fruit).
- **Post-workout:** protein + carb within a couple hours to support muscle repair.
- **Calories:** muscle building is generally supported by a small surplus — roughly 200-300 calories above maintenance — rather than an aggressive bulk.

## One-Page Summary

3x/week, 45-min full-body circuits using dumbbells and bands, built for muscle growth despite a fixed 25 lb dumbbell cap — overload comes from bands, tempo, and density instead of just adding weight. Weeks 1-3 build the base, 4-6 add real progression pressure, 7-9 push density and intensity, Week 10 deloads and retests.

## Top 3 Rules for Success

1. **Progress via band/tempo/rest once dumbbells stop being hard** — don't just keep doing the same 25 lb reps for 10 weeks.
2. **Protein and sleep matter as much as the workout** — muscle is built in recovery.
3. **Show up 3x/week even on light days** — consistency beats occasional heroics.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Sticking with 25 lb dumbbells indefinitely without adding bands/tempo once it's easy.
- Rushing circuits so fast that form breaks down.
- Under-eating protein while expecting muscle gain.
- Skipping warm-up because the session is short.

## After Week 10

Compare retest numbers to Week 1. If dumbbells + bands are consistently too easy even with tempo added, it's a good signal to invest in a heavier dumbbell set or add a resistance band with higher tension for the next block. Otherwise, repeat this structure with fresh baselines, or shift emphasis toward strength (lower reps, longer rest) if pure muscle gain has plateaued.
